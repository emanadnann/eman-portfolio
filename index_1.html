AlgoVista

A sorting and pathfinding visualiser that lets you watch algorithms think in real time. I built it to move beyond writing algorithms and actually see why Merge Sort's divide-and-conquer beats Bubble Sort's swaps, and how A* reaches the goal with less exploring than Dijkstra.

  • Bubble, Quick and Merge Sort animated step by step, with adjustable speed and array size
  • Dijkstra and A* search on a grid where you draw walls by clicking and dragging
  • Live comparison highlighting and shortest-path tracing, styled in custom CSS with no UI framework
